# Chicken site - food page list and competitor gap (25 Sep 2026)

Follows 11-backyard-chickens.md. Semrush US, include filter "chickens eat",
top 600 keywords by volume (down to 140/mo), grouped per food.

## Market for food questions

- Keywords containing "chickens eat": **6,792 keywords, 490,440 searches/month, avg KD 12.**
- Top 600 keywords group into **341 food variants (~200 distinct foods)**, 339,570 searches/month.
- Variants like peels, seeds, rinds, skins, tops, cooked/raw become sections (H2s) on
  the main food page, not separate pages (e.g. banana page covers banana peel 4,230).

## What this means for the build

- A bulk site can launch with **~200 food pages on day one**, vs yardroost's 44.
- Each page covers all variants (raw, cooked, peel, seeds, rind, leaves) of one food.
- One fixed page layout, filled from a food data table:
  answer (safe / in moderation / unsafe), why, how much, how to prepare, parts to avoid
  (e.g. rhubarb leaves, avocado skin and pit, green potato skins, apple seeds),
  nutrition (USDA FoodData Central), source links (university extension pages).
- The answer column must be written and checked by hand once per food. That is the real work.
- Then breeds (100+) and care pages (from 11-backyard-chickens.md).
