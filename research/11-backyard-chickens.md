## Data source

No single free API like the card sites.
- Foods: USDA FoodData Central (free API) for nutrition per food. The "safe / in
  moderation / unsafe" answer and the reason must be written once per food, then the
  robot builds the page. ~150-300 foods.
- Breeds: public lists (APA Standard of Perfection, Wikipedia breed list). Fields:
  origin, egg colour, eggs per year, size, temperament, cold/heat hardiness, broody.
  ~100 breeds plus colour varieties.
- Risk: animal health content. Every food answer must be correct and sourced
  (university extension pages, e.g. extension.umn.edu, canr.msu.edu).

## Risks

- Seasonal: chicken interest peaks in spring (chick season, Feb-May).
- Big old sites (backyardchickens.com 26y, mypetchicken.com 22y) own head terms.
  Win on long tail first, like yardroost.
- yardroost.com is itself new and growing; it is the direct competitor.
